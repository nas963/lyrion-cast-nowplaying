PLUGIN_CASTNOWPLAYING
	EN	Cast Now Playing
	SR	Cast Now Playing

PLUGIN_CASTNOWPLAYING_DESC
	EN	Shows the now playing screen (cover, title, artist, album, progress) on Chromecast and Google Cast TVs.
	SR	Prikazuje ekran sa pesmom koja svira (omot, naslov, izvođač, album, napredak) na Chromecast i Google Cast televizorima.

PLUGIN_CASTNOWPLAYING_DEVICES
	EN	Cast screens
	SR	Cast ekrani

PLUGIN_CASTNOWPLAYING_DEVICES_DESC
	EN	Turn on the screens that should show what is playing, pick the player each one follows, and give a screen your own name if you like. The screen is only taken when it is free: if another app is running, or the TV is off or on another input, nothing is shown until playback is started again.
	SR	Uključite ekrane na kojima treba da se vidi šta svira, izaberite plejer koji svaki prati i, po želji, dajte ekranu svoje ime. Ekran se preuzima samo kad je slobodan: ako radi druga aplikacija, ili je TV ugašen ili na drugom ulazu, ništa se ne prikazuje dok se reprodukcija ponovo ne pokrene.

PLUGIN_CASTNOWPLAYING_NONE_FOUND

	SR	Još nije pronađen nijedan Cast uređaj. Proverite da je TV ili boks upaljen i u istoj mreži, pa ponovo učitajte stranicu.
